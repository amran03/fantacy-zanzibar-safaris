ABOUT THE ISLAND

Zanzibar isn't just a holiday — it's a story living in every street and every wave

The island of Zanzibar, off the coast of Tanzania, blends Swahili, Arab and Indian culture in the narrow lanes of Stone Town — a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Beyond the city, you'll find turquoise water, spice farms scented with clove and cardamom, and the Jozani Forest, home to the rare Red Colobus monkey.

Our team of local guides knows every corner of the island — from quiet beaches on the east coast to the beautiful sunsets on Nungwi's western shore.
